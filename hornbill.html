A considered festival base

Camp close. Travel clearly. Stay in the story.

Hornbill Festival brings music, craft, food and cultural expression to Kisama. Northeast Basecamp shapes the practical parts around it: tent stays, arrival support, route coordination and social spaces that still feel grounded.

Festival-first location

Kisama Heritage Village is around 12 km south of Kohima; exact campsite access and pickup points are confirmed with each booking.

Travel-ready plans

Dimapur airport and railway station are the main arrival points. We coordinate campsite transfers and selected group routes.

More than a tent

Clean bedding, breakfast, washrooms on a scheduled cleaning plan, camp support, cafe time and shared evening spaces.

FAQ

Hornbill stay questions.

Are Hornbill Festival tickets included?

No. Festival entry tickets are separate unless a written package confirmation expressly says otherwise.

How do I reach the Basecamp?

Dimapur is the closest airport and railway entry point. Pickup timing, exact campsite access and transfer charges are confirmed for your selected plan.

What should I carry for Dzukou?

A light day bag, water, suitable walking shoes and warm layers. Participation is subject to weather, fitness and on-ground safety guidance.

What does the campsite include?

Included amenities are confirmed per stay category; plans typically include bedding, breakfast, shared washrooms on a cleaning schedule and campsite coordination.
